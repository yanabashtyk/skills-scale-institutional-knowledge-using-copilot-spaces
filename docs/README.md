# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the **OctoAcme Project Management documentation hub**. This collection of guides helps teams execute projects consistently, communicate clearly, and deliver customer value.

## Core Principles

OctoAcme's approach is built on five key principles:

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments rather than monolithic releases
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named leaders (PM and Product Manager) with defined responsibilities
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence and metrics
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## Project Lifecycle Overview

OctoAcme projects follow a structured lifecycle with clear phases, gates, and decision points:

1. **Initiation**: Problem statement, stakeholder alignment, high-level timeline
2. **Planning**: Scope definition, resource allocation, risk identification, backlog creation
3. **Execution**: Daily delivery, progress tracking, blocker management, team rhythm
4. **Release**: Deployment, verification, stakeholder communication, rollback readiness
5. **Retrospective**: Capture learnings, track improvements, refine processes

## How to Use These Docs

### For Team Members

1. **Starting a new project?** Begin with the [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) to validate business need and align stakeholders.
2. **Planning work?** Use the [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) guide to break work into increments and identify risks.
3. **In active delivery?** Reference the [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) guide for team rhythm, blockers, and quality practices.
4. **Preparing to release?** Follow the [Release & Deployment Guide](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) checklist to minimize risk.
5. **After a milestone or sprint?** Conduct a retrospective using the [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) guide.

### For New Team Members

1. Start with the [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) to understand OctoAcme's approach and roles.
2. Review [Personas & Roles](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) to understand how different functions contribute.
3. Explore the lifecycle phases above to see where your work fits.
4. Reference specific guides based on your project phase.

### For Project Managers

- Maintain the **Project Charter** and one-pager using the [Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) and [Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) templates
- Use the **Risk Register** from [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) to track and escalate risks
- Facilitate **weekly syncs** and standups as defined in [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)
- Schedule and run retrospectives using [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

### For Product Managers

- Define success metrics and acceptance criteria using [Initiation](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) and [Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) guides
- Prioritize backlog and communicate priorities to the delivery team
- Collaborate on trade-offs and scope with the PM and engineering leads
- Validate outcomes against success metrics defined in the Project One-pager

### For Developers

- Review acceptance criteria and Definition of Done from the backlog
- Follow branching and PR conventions documented in your project repo
- Contribute to estimates and risk identification during planning
- Participate in code reviews and maintain test coverage per [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily**: Team standups (15 min) — focus on progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly**: PM + PdM sync and delivery team sync — progress updates, risk review, decisions
- **Bi-weekly or Sprint**: Sprint/iteration planning and review/demo
- **Monthly**: Stakeholder updates with roadmap and progress
- **As needed**: Escalations, incident communication, and cross-team coordination
