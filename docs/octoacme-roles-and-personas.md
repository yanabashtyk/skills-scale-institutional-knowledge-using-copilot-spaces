# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Provides technical direction, makes architecture decisions, and ensures technical quality. Collaborates with developers and product leads to balance technical feasibility with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture proposals
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor junior developers on technical best practices
- Guide decisions on technology choices and integrations
- Participate in code review for critical components

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, well-documented technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt while delivering features
- Build team capability through mentoring and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code reviews and PR feedback on critical changes
- Technical risk discussions in planning and weekly syncs

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to review code and provide technical guidance
- Partners with **Product Managers** to ensure technical feasibility of proposed features
- Collaborates with **Project Managers** on technical risk assessment and timeline estimation
- Mentors team members and supports quality standards across projects

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
Owns quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. Ensures features meet acceptance criteria and that quality standards are maintained throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test strategy and test plans for features
- Define acceptance criteria with product and development teams
- Execute and coordinate acceptance testing
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Own release validation and smoke test execution
- Track defects and quality metrics

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, high-quality releases
- Catch defects early through effective test planning
- Reduce post-release issues and customer impact

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria workshops
- Defect triage and quality reviews
- Release coordination and post-deploy verification

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** to define clear acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Collaborates with **Developers** on testing strategy and defect triage
- Supports **Project Managers** with quality risk assessment and release readiness validation
- Works with **Technical Lead** on complex test scenarios and quality automation strategies

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Manages engineering team capacity, career development, and organizational structure. Ensures team health, skill growth, and resource allocation across projects while removing organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity and resource allocation across projects
- Support career development and professional growth of team members
- Conduct regular 1-on-1s and performance reviews
- Identify and resolve team-level bottlenecks and blockers
- Ensure team has skills needed for project success
- Foster psychological safety and team culture

### Goals
- Build a high-performing, motivated engineering team
- Develop next generation of technical leaders
- Reduce team turnover and burnout
- Enable consistent delivery through effective resource management

### Typical Communication
- One-on-one meetings with team members
- Project resource planning with Project Managers
- Performance and capability discussions with Product and Project Leads
- Team retrospectives and skill development planning

### How they interact with existing roles
- Supports **Developers** with mentoring, career growth, and removal of blockers
- Allocates team capacity and skills to meet **Project Manager** timelines
- Works with **Technical Lead** to ensure team has requisite technical skills
- Partners with **Product Managers** on capacity planning and team capability alignment

---

## Business Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Represents business and customer interests. Provides business context, priorities, and approval decisions. Ensures project delivers value aligned with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Approve project scope, timeline, and budget
- Escalate business-impacting risks and decisions
- Support stakeholder communication and alignment
- Validate that delivered features meet business objectives
- Remove business-level blockers and constraints

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and support
- Make timely decisions to unblock project progress

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and kickoff
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Decision gates and scope change approvals
- Escalation resolution and business risk discussions

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partners with **Product Managers** on business priorities and success metrics
- Provides **Project Managers** with business context, approvals, and escalation resolution
- Supports **Developers** and **Technical Lead** by removing business constraints and clarifying requirements
- Ensures team alignment on strategic business outcomes

---

## Design / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Ensures user experience and design consistency throughout projects. Collaborates with product and engineering teams to deliver intuitive, accessible, and visually cohesive solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Define design system and UX standards
- Create and review design mockups and prototypes
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Ensure accessibility and usability across features
- Collaborate on user flow and interaction patterns
- Review implementation against design specifications

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Maintain design consistency across all products
- Reduce user friction and support costs through great design
- Validate design decisions with user research

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights sharing
- Design specification and implementation reviews
- Collaboration on UX strategy and roadmap items

### How they interact with existing roles
- Works closely with **Product Managers** to define user needs and validate designs
- Partners with **Developers** to ensure design implementation fidelity
- Collaborates with **Technical Lead** on design system and technical implementation
- Supports **Project Managers** with UX risk assessment and acceptance criteria clarity

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When running projects, identify which personas are relevant to your team and clarify roles early in the project lifecycle.
